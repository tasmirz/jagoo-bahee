{\Large\bfseries Response to Reviewers}\hfill {\bfseries ICCIT 2026 \enspace/\enspace Paper 2056}\par
\vspace{3pt}
\textit{Islands of Reach: A Censorship-Resistant Federated Forum for National Internet Blackouts}\par

\reviewer{1}
\reply{1}{Multi-host validation}{Time constraints prevented us from evaluating the system across independent machines and routers for this revision. All nodes shared one physical host, and we limited our claims to that testbed. Multi-host evaluation, including clock skew measurement, remains future work (Secs.~IV, VI--VII).}
\reply{2}{Repeated measurements}{All 20 fresh chains arrived before the single route withdrawal, and all 20 issued afterward arrived on the same stack. Post medians were 0.735 and 1.732~s, with 95\% bootstrap intervals of 0.507--0.913 and 1.482--1.841~s. We also report IQRs and sample standard deviations (Secs.~IV, V-A, Fig.~5).}
\reply{3}{Network impairments}{All ten posts arrived in each of six conditions on a fixed bridge path, including the unshaped control (median 1.154~s). Tests covered delay and jitter, loss, a bandwidth cap, and intermittent outages. All ten outage-test posts were queued before the first recovery (median 18.595~s). Path diversity remains untested (Secs.~IV, V-A, Fig.~5).}
\reply{4}{End-to-end baseline}{On the same shaped link, both systems delivered 20/20 posts. Median latency was 521~ms from source to destination database insertion for Jagoo Bahee and 155~ms from the minimal inbox's send routine to verified in-memory receipt. Container memory snapshots were 88--92 and 9--14~MiB, respectively. With inbound ports blocked, pull recovered 10/10 and push delivered 0/10 within its retry budget (Secs.~IV, V-C, Table~III).}
\reply{5}{Admission under load}{We added valid-write, duplicate, nonce-replay, malformed-input, and five-peer quota tests. The receiver rejected all 500 peer frames, including 407 with bad-signature errors. The over-quota rounds blocked two peers. Sustained resource-exhaustion thresholds remain unmeasured (Secs.~V-B, VI, Table~II).}
\reply{6}{Accountability}{Receipts and inclusion proofs bind the content identifier, which excludes the author signature. Independent audit-log services verify the receipt and inclusion proof and check the author signature separately. Local consistency proofs are available over HTTP. Refusal before admission leaves no receipt (Sec.~III-D, Table~I).}
\reply{7}{Decoder validation}{TypeScript, Rust and Python agreed on 10,000 generated wire-format cases, including numeric boundaries and Unicode strings. Across 16 mutation classes, all three decoders rejected the same 9,455 cases and accepted the other 545. An earlier run exposed a 32-bit bounds error that we fixed and covered with a regression test (Sec.~V-A).}
\reply{8}{Figures and language}{We redrew the admission stages and route topology (Figs.~2--3), labeled hop count, latency units and variability (Fig.~4), and edited the manuscript for clarity.}

\reviewer{2}
\reply{1}{Testbed scope}{The abstract and conclusion limit the findings to the single-host experiments. Section~VI identifies physical routing, inter-host clock skew, variable access links, and wide-area latency as open evaluation questions.}
\reply{2}{Latency variability}{Section~V-A gives post IQRs and standard deviations, and Fig.~5 gives 95\% intervals for medians. Each scaling configuration used 50 posts, observed at successive hops (Fig.~4, Sec.~V-B).}
\reply{3}{Resources}{The eight-node run used 77--111~MiB per application container, with a sampled CPU maximum of 21.18\%. Shared MongoDB and Redis used 184 and 9~MiB. Peak RSS, disk usage and single-board-computer performance remain unmeasured (Secs.~V-B, VI).}
\reply{4}{Transport timeouts}{Default deadlines are 5~s for control, 15~s for delivery and 30~s for backfill. Delivery cancels the RPC on timeout. Retry delays double from 1~s to a 15~min cap (Sec.~III-C). The ISP gate and inbox comparison used the rebuilt transport. The route, impairment, stress and scaling runs preceded it (Sec.~IV).}
\reply{5}{Concurrency}{All 200 posts per setting reached the local projection. At 1, 4, 16 and 32 concurrent requests, throughput was 29, 27, 9 and 6 requests/s, respectively. The 95th-percentile latency rose from 49~ms to 12.587~s (Sec.~V-B, Table~II).}
\reply{6}{ActivityPub sizes}{We measured 40 synthetic posts on a local Mastodon 4.7.6 instance in Docker. Median object sizes were 3,385~B raw and 811.5~B gzipped. In the separate transport test, sender-interface traffic averaged 1,846~B/post for Jagoo Bahee and 1,278~B/post for the minimal inbox (Sec.~V-C, Table~III).}
\reply{7}{Fork detection}{Peers compare nonstale heads with the expected server key and flag different roots at equal size or a newer, smaller tree. Peer observation lacks signature and append-only growth verification. Isolated observers can receive conflicting views (Sec.~III-D, Table~I).}
\reply{8}{Language and conference fit}{We edited the paper for clarity. The introduction presents resilient federation over surviving IP paths, and Section~III-D explains accountability through signed admission receipts.}
