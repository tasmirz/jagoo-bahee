# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users

Jagoo serves people participating in pseudonymous communities when ordinary connectivity may be
slow, filtered, monitored, or absent. They need familiar forum workflows for reading, publishing,
moderating, messaging, and verifying content without learning security jargon. Core flows must stay
clear one-handed, offline, on low-end or damaged phones, and in Bangla or English.

### Brand Personality

**Federated. Unstoppable. Anonymous.** The interface should also feel safe, secure, private, calm,
and candid. It should communicate exactly what is verified, queued, hidden, or unreachable without
using fear, surveillance imagery, or theatrical “hacker” styling.

### Aesthetic Direction

Follow `Plans/design.md` and the supplied light/dark visual board. Use a refined, information-dense
forum shell with Poppins typography, JetBrains Mono only for cryptographic evidence, quiet gray
surfaces, Ember for pseudonymous community space, and Signal blue only for identified messaging.
The Seal, Reach Pill, moderation banner, transport tag, cost ring, and explicit queue state are the
signature devices. Gradients appear only in onboarding and successful sync feedback. Avoid generic
social-media gloss, lifestyle-app decoration, neon cyberpunk, pervasive glassmorphism, and
alarm-colored routine moderation.

### Design Principles

1. Make trust, provenance, and reach ambient on every relevant screen, never modal or hidden.
2. Preserve the Ember/Signal boundary so the two identity planes cannot appear visually linked.
3. Render cached content first and describe queued, stale, constrained, and blackout states plainly.
4. Keep every action usable without color, imagery, animation, fine motor control, or English.
5. Prefer restrained hierarchy, dividers, and spacing over nested cards or decorative effects.
6. Treat `Plans/design.md` as the token and component authority and the supplied board as the
   visual-fidelity reference.
